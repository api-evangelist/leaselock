# LeaseLock

> LeaseLock is an insurtech platform for rental housing that replaces the traditional cash security deposit with lease insurance. Its Zero Deposit program embeds coverage into the lease via a signed addendum, while LeaseLock Shield applies AI to ledger data, market trends and lease profiles to underwrite risk for property owners and operators. LeaseLock publishes no public developer API; integration with property management systems is arranged through the company.
